About us

We believe small spaces deserve big ideas.

We started Made by Sundays because we couldn't find what we needed anywhere. The console table for the back of our (very standard) couch didn't exist. The desk that hid when work was done didn't exist. The pieces were either too big, too small, or simply not designed in a way that would fit our needs.

So we built what we needed. Then realised other people could be looking for the same thing.

Made by Sundays designs and builds bespoke furniture that makes the best of your space, in the most thoughtful and beautiful way. We turn dead corners into storage, awkward alcoves into features, and rooms that do too much into spaces that finally work.

And if you have a Pinterest-inspired idea you couldn't find in any store — we build that too. Made to your space, your way, by Sundays.
